#+title: auto-opti.tb-var — runnable example

A =tb-var= (time-based variable) stores a value at a =bucket= (a natural
number representing simulation time). Two semantics ship:

- *additive* — =measure= adds to whatever is already at that bucket
  (e.g. production recorded over time).
- *latest* — =measure= sets the value from that bucket onward until the
  next measure (e.g. a stock level).

Each comes in a =deltas= (sparse) and =contiguous= (dense) storage form.

Source of truth: [[file:tb_var.clj][tb_var.clj]]. Run all examples with =bb examples=.

#+begin_src clojure
(require '[auto-opti :as-alias opti])
(require '[auto-opti.tb-var :as tb-var])

;; Additive: two measures at bucket 10 accumulate (3 + 2); bucket 11 gets 4.
(def production
  (-> (tb-var/tb-var-additive-deltas)
      (tb-var/measure 10 3)
      (tb-var/measure 10 2)
      (tb-var/measure 11 4)))
(tb-var/get-measure production 10)                    ;; => 5
(tb-var/get-measures production (range 8 14))         ;; => [0 0 5 4 0 0]

;; Latest: a measure holds until the next one; default before any.
(def stock
  (-> (tb-var/tb-var-latest-deltas :empty)
      (tb-var/measure 10 3)
      (tb-var/measure 11 4)))
(tb-var/get-measures stock (range 8 14))              ;; => [:empty :empty 3 4 4 4]

;; Aggregator: group buckets [10;20[ into steps of 5, store on aggregates.
(def aggregated
  (-> [#::opti{:start-bucket 10 :end-bucket 20 :step 5}]
      tb-var/aggregator
      (tb-var/tb-var-aggregated (tb-var/tb-var-additive-deltas))
      (tb-var/measure 10 3)
      (tb-var/measure 14 4)))
(mapv #(tb-var/get-measure aggregated %) [9 10 11 14 15 16])  ;; => [nil 7 7 7 0 0]
#+end_src

#+RESULTS:
: additive @10       : 5
: additive 8..13     : [0 0 5 4 0 0]
: latest 8..13       : [:empty :empty 3 4 4 4]
: aggregated 9..16   : [nil 7 7 7 0 0]
