#+title: auto-opti.sample — runnable example

=auto-opti.sample= provides descriptive statistics over plain collections
of numbers: average, variance, standard deviation, median, midrange,
frequencies. Useful to summarize the results of a batch of simulation
draws.

Source of truth: [[file:sample.clj][sample.clj]]. Run all examples with =bb examples=.

#+begin_src clojure
(require '[auto-opti.sample :as sample])

(def xs [2 4 4 4 5 5 7 9])

(sample/n xs)                          ;; => 8
(sample/average xs)                    ;; => 5
(sample/variance xs)                   ;; => 4.0
(sample/standard-deviation xs)         ;; => 2.0
(sample/median [1 10 14 17 2000])      ;; => 14   (odd count: middle element)
(sample/median [1 10 20 1000])         ;; => 15   (even count: mean of middle two)
(sample/midrange [2 98 13 10])         ;; => 50   ((min + max) / 2)
#+end_src
