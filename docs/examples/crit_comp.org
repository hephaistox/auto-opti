#+title: auto-opti.crit-comp — runnable example

A criteria comparator decides whether solution 1 is =:better=, =:worst= or
=:equal= to solution 2 given their criteria maps. =crit-comp-fn= looks a
strategy up in =default-registry= by =:crit-comp-name= and returns a ready
comparator function =(fn [crit1 crit2] ...)=.

This =.org= is the runnable source: =bb examples= executes the block below.

#+begin_src clojure :results output :exports both
(require '[auto-opti.crit-comp :as crit-comp])

(let [sol-a {:cost 12 :delay 2 :quality 100}
      sol-b {:cost 13 :delay 2 :quality 100}
      ;; Hierarchical: rank by :cost first, then :delay (smaller is better).
      hierarchical (crit-comp/crit-comp-fn
                    #:auto-opti{:crit-comp-name :hierarchise
                                :order [#:auto-opti{:crit-comp-name :smaller :crit-name :cost}
                                        #:auto-opti{:crit-comp-name :smaller :crit-name :delay}]})
      ;; Weighted-sum: combine criteria with weights, smaller sum is better.
      weighted (crit-comp/crit-comp-fn
                #:auto-opti{:crit-comp-name :weighted-sum :weights {:cost 10 :delay 100}})
      smaller (crit-comp/crit-comp-fn #:auto-opti{:crit-comp-name :smaller})]
  (println "=== auto-opti.crit-comp ===")
  (println "hierarchise a vs b :" (hierarchical sol-a sol-b))
  (println "weighted a vs b    :" (weighted {:cost 12 :delay 2} {:cost 13 :delay 4}))
  (println "smaller 12 vs 20   :" (smaller 12 20)))
#+end_src
